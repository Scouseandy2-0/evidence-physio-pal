## Required review entry for each completed item

- Item ID/version and the source/version/date actually checked.
- Reviewer name, professional role and relevant scope (only record with their knowledge; none has been invented).
- Accuracy, wording, uncertainty, missing material, accessibility and source-location findings.
- Corrections made, unresolved limits and intended audience/use.
- Review date, next review trigger/date and explicit delivery state.

Material changed after approval needs proportionate re-review. A source being linked, a test passing or an editable copy being saved must never automatically produce a reviewed/approved label.
