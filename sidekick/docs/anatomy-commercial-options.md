The page requests contact for pricing; no defensible monthly cost was available.

Before choosing it, obtain a written offer for a paid physio/student app, confirm end-user and model-view charging (including repeat launches), redistribution/branding terms, source/content maintenance, muscle origins/insertions and bone-landmark coverage, API selection events and a prototype with our persistent panel, iPhone performance, offline/export rights and termination/data portability. Public product features do not establish these contractual terms.

Visible Body and Complete Anatomy remain interaction/content benchmarks. Their standard consumer subscriptions have not been established as commercial embedding licences for PhysioSidekick. No proprietary model, screenshot or content from those services has been imported.

Decision for this build: deliver the source-linked local inspector using the existing licensed geometry. Keep the wider-atlas licensing decision open until a specific quote and technical demonstration are available; do not imply a BioDigital integration is already present.

## Completeness reassessment after Andrew’s clarification

The current 177 grouped entries are not an accepted substitute for whole-body individual-muscle coverage. A candidate must provide an auditable structure/side/head inventory plus actual attachment/action/innervation content and useful bone information, not just a larger mesh file. Region-on-demand loading should preserve complete catalogue coverage while respecting phone performance.

The official Z-Anatomy repository was inspected: https://github.com/Z-Anatomy/Models-of-human-anatomy/blob/master/Readme.md and https://github.com/Z-Anatomy/Models-of-human-anatomy/blob/master/License.txt . It supplies a Blender atlas/template derived from BodyParts3D under stated share-alike terms, with definitions relying heavily on Wikipedia. Its attribution also lists separately licensed noncommercial inner-ear/kidney assets and warns about translation errors. Therefore it is a candidate for a carefully audited MSK subset, not a blanket-cleared commercial package or a verified source for a complete attachment database. No models or definitions from it were incorporated in this repair.

BioDigital remains a candidate for commercial atlas integration, with terms and exact coverage still requiring a written quote/technical evaluation. An authorised enquiry was sent on 27 November 2026 from theconfidentphysio@gmail.com to developers@biodigital.com, requesting technical suitability, comprehensive coverage, mobile interaction, commercial rights, startup pricing and a guided evaluation, with referral to sales as appropriate. Gmail confirmed the message as SENT. A reply and quote are pending; no paid commitment has been made.
